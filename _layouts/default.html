Yuanzhe Liu

I am an incoming Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. Previously, I received my M.S. in Data Science from the University of Pennsylvania and my B.S. in Mathematics from the University of Wisconsin-Madison.

My research focuses on generative models and reinforcement learning for embodied robotics, with the goal of enabling embodied agents to perceive, reason, and plan in the physical world. I hope to solve real-world challenges, transforming cutting-edge research into practical, scalable solutions.
